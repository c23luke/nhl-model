How it works

Every shot gets a goal chance from where it was taken and how: distance, angle, shot type, rebound, rush, cross-ice pass, strength. Every player is rated from his own history wherever he played it, and tonight's team is the players who actually dress plus the goalie who actually starts. No team-season stat goes in.

The board runs twice a day:

  1. Morning preview with projected goalies and lines. Picks show as waiting.
  2. Final run about 60–90 minutes before puck drop, after goalies are confirmed. Picks that pass every check turn amber. Bet those at the book shown. It has the best payout after fees across DraftKings, Bovada, Novig, ProphetX, Kalshi, Polymarket and BetOpenly; the other books are listed in order as backups. One unit is to win $20. If the best book has a cap below that, the board fills what it can there and puts the rest at the next-best book. Kalshi and Polymarket charge a fee on each contract and ProphetX takes 2% of winnings, so a price that looks equal can pay less there.

Each pick saves the price you got and the closing price. Beating the closing price is the fastest sign an angle is real.

Backtest records come from three seasons (2023-24 to 2025-26) at prices about 15 minutes before puck drop, using only information available before each game.
